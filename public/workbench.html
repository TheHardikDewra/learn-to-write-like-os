The workbench

Write. Get measured against him.

Every band on the right is measured from his live voice - the last twelve posts he published, October 2025 to August 2026. That is deliberately not his books: his 2026 sentences run about 14 words against 16.6 in his 2024 blog, and nearly one in three is now eight words or shorter. He is still tightening. Green means you are inside his current range. This does not check whether your writing is good, it checks whether it has his shape. Shape is the part you can drill.
