The programme

Eight hours a day, twelve weeks

You said you would give it eight hours. Here is what eight hours looks like when it is aimed rather than merely spent. Three phases: strip, build, then voice. The order matters - subtraction first, because deletion needs no talent and it is what makes room for everything else.

How to run this

Do the day in order. The break is not optional. Never skip Block 4 to do more of Block 3 - drills without shipping produce a technician who has never faced a reader. On a day you can only give two hours, do Block 1, Block 3, and Block 7.
