Why no Sanskrit tier

You speak Hindi and you already know prakriti, purusha, maya, sattva, rajas, tamas, rishi and bhava. Teaching you those would be filler. Every Indic term, every proper noun and every mythological name was stripped out. What is left is English you can put in a client email.
